Hands-free

Wake it with a word.

Turn on Listen for “Oy” and Parrot listens on the microphone you pick. Start a sentence with the wake word and it types the rest.

  1. Say “Oy” at the start of a sentence. Saying it mid-sentence doesn't count, so talking about parrots won't set it off.
  2. Keep talking. A soft sound plays and a red dot appears on the parrot in the menu bar.
  3. Pause for a second. Parrot writes it down on your Mac and types it into the field you're in.

Prefer another word? Pick any word in settings. “Parrot” works too.

How it works

Everything stays on your Mac.

Tap ⌥ to start and stop

No wake word needed. Tap Option, speak, tap it again. Option shortcuts still work, because only a quick tap on its own counts.

Local speech models

Parakeet models run on the Neural Engine. Your voice never leaves your Mac, and you can switch models from the menu bar.

Pastes where you are

Parrot types into the field you're in, then puts your clipboard back. Apple's on-device model fixes punctuation and drops the ums.

Your vocabulary

Add names and jargon, with how they get misheard, and Parrot spells them your way.

Call notes

Record a call from the menu bar. You get a transcript of both sides and a summary with decisions and action items.

Searchable history

Every dictation, with the model used and how long it took. Compare models on your own recordings.
